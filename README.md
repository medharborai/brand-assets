## How to write the name

- Write **MedHarbor**: one word, capital M and capital H.
- Not "Medharbor", "Med Harbor", "MEDHARBOR" or "medharbor" in running text.
- The domain is written in lower case: **medharborai.com**.

## Please do not change the logo

- Do not redraw, stretch, rotate or recolour the logo or the mark.
- Do not add effects such as shadows or outlines.
- Keep clear space around the logo, at least the height of the cross in the mark.
- Use `logo.svg` on light backgrounds and `logo-on-dark.svg` on dark backgrounds.

If you need a format that is not here, email [info@medharborai.com](mailto:info@medharborai.com).
